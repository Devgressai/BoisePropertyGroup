        Plan Map and Text Amendment and Zoning Ordinance Text Amendment to Title 9 of the Ada
        County Code to reestablish the City of Boise Area of Impact and update the City of Boise
        Comprehensive Plan and Future Land Use Map, adopted on July 10, 2024. Stacey DuPuis,
        Community and Regional Planner, at either (208) 287-7944 or sdupuis@adacounty.id.gov.

        R. Beck opened the public hearing.

        S. DuPuis presented the Staff report.

        Carol Vidales testified in opposition to the application.

        Carlos Vidales presented testimony and a PowerPoint in opposition to the application,
        expressing concerns about sewer services and requesting that lifting the City of Boise’s sewer
        moratorium be added as a condition of approval.

        for annual review as an additional condition of approval.

        Nick Nettles testified in opposition to the application, repeating previous concerns raised and
        regarding sewer connections.

        Doug Miller testified he would support the application with the additional condition of approval
        removing the sewer moratorium.

        Colin Nash gave testimony in support of the application.

     7. 202501571-CU-MSP-V, DESRI: Conditional Use and Master Site Plan for a Centralized
        Power Facility (Solar Farm), Electric Transmission Line, Battery Energy Storage System and
        Substation. Also included is a Variance to maximum site coverage. The property is generally
        located north of Kuna Mora Rd and east of S Cole Rd and contains approximately 2,600 acres
        in the Rural Preservation (RP) District; Sections 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33, 34, 35, T.2N, R.2E
        and Section 3, T.1N, R.2E. Michaella Owens, Associate Planner, at either (208) 287-7924 or
        mowens@adacounty.id.gov.

         R. Beck opened the public hearing.

         M. Owens presented the Staff report.

         Caitlin Clancy, representing Applicant, presented testimony and a PowerPoint supporting the
         application.

         Adam Evan testified in opposition to the application, expressing fire hazard concerns.

         Erin Evan gave testimony in opposition regarding environmental concerns, setback distances,
         construction dust, noise and traffic.

         Jen Odell reiterated previous testimony.

         Chris Maloney testified in opposition to the application regarding solar farm maintenance.

         Carol Vidales gave opposing testimony echoing concerns for fire safety.

         Hannah Berry reiterated prior testimony.

         C. Clancy, Meghan Knudsen and Paul Brown gave rebuttal testimony addressing concerns
         raised.

         The parties discussed fire suppression plans and decommissioning requirements.

         R. Beck closed the public hearing.

             ACTION:         T. DAYLEY MOVED TO APPROVE APPLICATION NO.
                             202501571-CU-MSP-V, DESRI, WITH REVISED CONDITIONS OF
                             APPROVAL TO CLARIFY DECOMMISSIONING FINANCIAL
                             PLAN, FIRE PLAN AND SETBACKS AS DISCUSSED; AND

                             MEETING FOR REVISED FINDINGS OF FACT AND
                             CONCLUSIONS OF LAW. R. DAVIDSON SECONDED.
