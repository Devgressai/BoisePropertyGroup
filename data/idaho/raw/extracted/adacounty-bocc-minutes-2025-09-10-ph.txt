       -   202402581-ZOA, ADA COUNTY DEVELOPMENT SERVICES: Zoning Ordinance Text
           Amendment to repeal and replace Title 8 of the Ada County Code - Ada County Zoning.
           Richard Beck, Planning Director at (208) 287-7915 or rbeck@adacounty.id.gov (Tabled from
           07/30/25; Public Hearing Closed)

           Richard Beck gave a brief overview of the application.

           The Board discussed the zoning ordinance rewrite process with Staff, reviewed and deliberated
           on each of the changes recommended by the Planning and Zoning Commission and by Staff,
           and directed Staff to draft additional changes.

               ACTION:         R. DAVIDSON MOVED TO APPROVE APPLICATION NO.
                               202402581-ZOA, ADA COUNTY DEVELOPMENT SERVICES;
                               AND TABLE IT TO THE OCTOBER 14, 2025, OPEN BUSINESS
                               MEETING TO ALLOW STAFF TIME TO INCORPORATE THE
                               REVISIONS RECOMMENDED BY THE BOARD. T. DAYLEY
                               SECONDED. R. DAVIDSON, AYE, T. DAYLEY, AYE, AND R.
                               BECK, AYE. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.
