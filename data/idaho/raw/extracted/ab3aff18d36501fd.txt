Selling the Home
You may need to sell the home to repay the HECM loan. If the loan balance is
more than the home is worth, the estate or heirs may sell the home for at least
95-percent of the current appraised value and the lender will accept the net
proceeds as satisfaction of the loan. Any post death transfer is considered a “sale”
for these purposes.

Keeping the Home
If you prefer to keep the home instead of selling it, the HECM loan balance must
be paid off in full.

Take Action Today
Contact the HECM lender immediately to review your responsibilities and options.
