     9. 202402701-CPA-PC, SVP LLC: A Planned Community Comprehensive Plan Map and Text
        Amendment for the Shadow Valley Planned Community, which will consist of approximately
        395 homes on 560 acres. The subject property is generally located east of Highway 55 between
        Spring Creek Way and Brookside Lane, and west of Highway 55 north of High Valley Way
        within the Rural Residential (RR) and Rural Preservation (RP) Districts; Sections 13, 24, 25,
        26, T.5N, R.1E. Brent Danielson, Senior Planner, at either (208) 287-7913 or
        bdanielson@adacounty.id.gov.

        R. Beck opened the public hearing.

        B. Danielson presented the Staff report.

        The Board and Staff discussed concerns raised in agency and public comments and at the
        Planning and Zoning Commission public hearing.

        Applicant presented testimony supporting the application and addressing concerns.

        The parties discussed water usage and resources, sewage treatment, school bus parking, public
        service standard waivers, commercial properties, residential density, the golf course, highway
        access and traffic.

        Randy Hurst testified in opposition to the application stating water and traffic concerns.

        B. Danielson explained the planned community application process.

        Attorney Josh Leonard, Clark Wardle LLP, gave opposing testimony and presented a
        PowerPoint.

        J. Leonard and the Board discussed Boise Hunter Homes requesting reimbursement from
        Applicant for improvements made to Highway 55, and water resources.

        Saxon Poppell gave testimony opposing the application regarding density and traffic.

        Derek Poppell reiterated prior testimony in opposition to the application.

        Jerry Troy gave testimony in opposition to the application regarding water and density.

        Karen Brodesser echoed previous opposing testimony.

        Ben Schultz gave opposing testimony reiterating concerns raised.

        Callie Kelley testified in opposition to the application voicing similar concerns.

        Matt Lorenz gave testimony in opposition regarding impact of wildfires, fire safety issues and
        lack of egress.

        Roxanne Licht reiterated previous testimony in opposition to the application.

        Rachel Artner testified in opposition repeating previous concerns.

        Natasha German echoed previous testimony in opposition.

        Ryan Stadler gave testimony in opposition reiterating concerns.

        Celeste Haase echoed previous opposing testimony.

        Applicant gave rebuttal testimony.

        The parties discussed the concerns raised.

        R. Beck closed the public hearing.
