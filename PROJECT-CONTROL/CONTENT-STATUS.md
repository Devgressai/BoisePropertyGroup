## Quality gate (Phase 39 of the brief)

A route becomes indexable only when it has: unique intro · minimum evidence ·
valid parent · valid related links · unique metadata · valid schema · sufficient
depth · primary entity present · no unsupported claims · no duplicate H1 · no
California residue · no placeholder · renders · correct sitemap membership.

## Copy rules

- No SEO filler, no empty introductions, no "Whether you're…", "In today's…",
  "Navigating the complexities…", "Look no further…", no fake empathy.
- Precise entities over "our area" / "the region" / "local homeowners".
- One claim, one section.
- No legal advice; procedural information with sources.
- Margin never labelled "profit", never shown as a markup percentage.
