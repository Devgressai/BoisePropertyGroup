## Could not source / left out
- **ITD manufactured-home titling.** ITD publishes no manufactured-home title page (site search and
  the DMV menus checked). The vehicle titles page never mentions manufactured homes. It is used only
  because 49-501(2)(b) applies the chapter's titling requirements to them, so those three claims are
  MEDIUM. ITD forms (title application, bill of sale, Conditional Title Statement of Facts) were not
  read; the prose does not describe their steps. The Ada County DMV forms page has nothing
  manufactured-home-specific.
- **49-502 / 49-503** (seller must deliver title with assignment; buyer acquires no interest until a
  title issues). Read and cached, but both are worded for "vehicles". Whether they reach manufactured
  homes through 49-501 is an attorney's call, so they were not claimed.
- **Tax Commission.** Its only manufactured-home tax guidance found is a 1997 letter (EIS00453) built
  on a 63-201 personal-property definition that no longer appears in the current statute, plus a 2006
  appraisal memo. Both too old to cite. Cached and extracted anyway.
- **Moving permits / tax receipt for transport.** Not researched in a primary source; left out.
- **HUD label replacement / label verification letters.** No live HUD page found (old mhs URLs 404).
  Only the eCFR standards are cited.
- **55-2009A** (lienholder notice, 60-day back-rent cap) and **55-2010** (closure notice to prospective
  purchasers, 90-day non-renewal) were read. 55-2010's closure paragraph was drafted then cut for
  length; it would be a good later addition. 55-2008(2) (rule changes on home type/size do not apply
  to a compliant resident) was left out because it does not say whether it protects a buyer.

## Traps
- 55-2009(1) protects against denial or removal "solely on the basis of the sale". Never shorten to
  "the park cannot stop a sale" or "the buyer can stay".
- 55-2009(4) five working days runs from a WRITTEN application. The statute gives no consequence for
  missing it; do not invent a deemed approval.
- 55-2007(2)(b): entrance/exit fees barred, but required repairs as a condition of approval are not
  such fees. Keep both halves together.
- 55-2004: the Act does not apply where the landlord rents both lot and home.
- 63-904 does not name manufactured homes. The link to them is the Ada Assessor's statement that an
  undeclared home is taxed as personal property. Keep the two attributed separately.
- "A used manufactured home is not subject to Idaho's sales or use tax" is the Assessor's sentence,
  inside its declaration steps. No statute read. Attributed, MEDIUM.
- 63-701: "a manufactured home MAY be a homestead". Not a statement that a home on a rented lot
  qualifies for the exemption.
- ITD fee table already shows a county change dated 2026-11-19; Ada's $21.00 must be re-checked before
  publishing.
- 39-4105 was amended in 2026 (ch. 171); current page shows one version.
